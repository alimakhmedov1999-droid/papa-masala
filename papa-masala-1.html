Our story

Family recipes, food-truck fast

Papa Masala started as a family kitchen habit — the masala fries Dad made after every cricket match — and turned into a window on Kent Kangley Road where that same plate gets handed to you in minutes.

We fold Indian spice work into the sandwiches, burgers, and fries people already crave, so nothing on the menu needs an introduction. Order at the window, grab a stool on the patio, and dig in while it's hot.
